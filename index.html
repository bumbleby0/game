How to play

  • Use Arrow keys / WASD or on-screen buttons to move one tile at a time.
  • Avoid cars on road rows — hitting a car respawns you.
  • At the top row there are two gates (left & right). Go through the gate labelled with the correct answer.
  • Correct +10 points, wrong -5. Best score saved locally.
